Prospective PhD students

We are always glad to hear from strong candidates interested in wind energy, atmospheric turbulence and environmental fluid mechanics — including those bringing a background in applied mathematics, machine learning or data science rather than classical fluid dynamics. Send a CV and a short note on what interests you to whichever investigator's work is closest to your own; their addresses are just above.
