Our work runs from the aerodynamics of a single rotor through to the systems that get wind capacity built and delivered. What holds it together is a preference for models that are simple enough to reason about and fast enough to use — grounded in the physics, but built for the problems industry actually faces. Models developed here are implemented in FLORIS (NREL) and PyWake (DTU).
